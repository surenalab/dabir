SurenalabGitHub

Tools for people who do research, made by one of them

Surenalab is the studio of Mohammad Sadegh Salehi, a machine learning researcher. It builds small, careful software for the parts of research that the big tools leave to chance: writing the paper, keeping the code and the figures honest, and working with coauthors and AI agents without giving your work to a server.

Dabir

A local-first desktop editor for scientific papers. LaTeX, the code behind the figures, Git, live coauthors and an AI agent you review, in one window on your own machine. Free and open source, for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Open Dabir

The name is Surena, the Parthian general, with a lab attached. Everything here is published under the MIT licence, and the repositories carry the full history of how it was made.
